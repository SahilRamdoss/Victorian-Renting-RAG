<div class="content rte-content">
    <p>Rental providers (landlords) and agents must give renters this guide on or before the day they move in. You can read more about renting guides at&nbsp;<a href="/housing/renting/starting-and-changing-rental-agreements/resources-and-guides-for-renters/resources-and-guides-overview">Resources and guides overview</a>.</p>
<h3>Introduction</h3>
<p>Victoria has some of the strongest rental protections in Australia, but&nbsp;renting can still be complicated. With 1 in 3 Victorians now renting, understanding your rental rights has never been more important.</p>
<p>The <em>Renters Guide</em> explains the rules and your rights at every stage&nbsp;of renting in Victoria, from applying for a property to moving out.</p>
<p>Whether you&rsquo;re a first-time renter or have rented before, this guide&nbsp;provides you with the information you need to rent confidently.</p>
<h3>Key terms</h3>
<ul>
    <li><strong>Rental provider:</strong>&nbsp;landlord.</li>
    <li><strong>Renter:</strong>&nbsp;tenant.</li>
    <li><strong>Agent:</strong>&nbsp;real estate agent.</li>
    <li><strong>The Residential Tenancies Act 1997:</strong>&nbsp;This sets out the laws, rights&nbsp;and responsibilities of renters and rental providers in Victoria.</li>
    <li><strong>RTBA:</strong> the Residential Tenancies Bond Authority. It holds residential rental bonds for Victorians as a neutral party, for the renter and the rental provider.</li>
    <li><strong>PRBS: </strong>Portable Rental Bond Scheme. Eligible renters can transfer the value of their existing bond or &lsquo;bond money&rsquo; to their next rental property, saving them being out of pocket.</li>
    <li><strong>RDRV:</strong>&nbsp;Rental Dispute Resolution Victoria. A free dispute resolution service&nbsp;to help renters and rental providers resolve disputes.</li>
    <li><strong>VCAT:</strong>&nbsp;Victorian Civil and Administrative Tribunal. This tribunal resolves&nbsp;legal disputes and cases in Victoria, including renting issues.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Where to find more information</h3>
<p>Our website has&nbsp;detailed resources on:</p>
<ul>
    <li><a href="/housing/renting">General renting information</a>&nbsp;(for renters, rental providers and agents)</li>
    <li><a href="/resources-and-tools/other-languages">Information in other languages</a>&nbsp;</li>
    <li><a href="/resources-and-tools/consumers-with-a-disability/easy-english-factsheets">Easy English resources</a>.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Stay updated</h3>
<p>For the most current information, go to&nbsp;<a href="/housing/renting">Renting</a>.</p>
<p>Follow Consumer Affairs Victoria on&nbsp;<a href="https://www.facebook.com/ConsumerAffairsVictoria">Facebook</a>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<a href="https://www.instagram.com/consumeraffairsvictoria">Instagram</a>&nbsp;for regular&nbsp;renting information and updates.</p>
