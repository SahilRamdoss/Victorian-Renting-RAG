<div class="content rte-content">
    Learn about finding and applying for a rental property in Victoria:
<h3></h3>
</div>


<div id="page_content_1_subl_middlecolumn_1" class="section accordion ">
        
    <a class="accordion-toggle collapsed" aria-controls="{528FAB5E-503D-42BD-A17C-E4B01A827D8B}" aria-expanded="false" href="#">
        <h3 class="heading">Before you apply</h3>
    </a>              
        
    <div id="{528FAB5E-503D-42BD-A17C-E4B01A827D8B}" class="accordion-content">
        <h3>Documents and information you may need to provide:</h3>
<ul>
    <li>Formal identification (such as a driver&rsquo;s licence)</li>
    <li>Employment and financial details,  such as a payslip</li>
    <li>Rental or residential history</li>
    <li>Personal references, if requested.</li>
</ul>
<p>Rental providers must ask for this information through the prescribed rental application form. They can&rsquo;t ask you for any information that is not set out in the form.</p>
<p>Read more about <a href="/housing/renting/starting-and-changing-rental-agreements/applying-signing-and-moving-in/applying-for-a-property">applying for a property</a>.</p>
<p>The rental provider or agent cannot ask you for the following information in your application:</p>
<ul>
    <li>Whether you&rsquo;ve taken legal action or had a dispute with a previous rental provider.</li>
    <li>Your bond history, including any claims you&rsquo;ve made on your bond.</li>
    <li>Detailed bank statements with daily transactions (if you need to provide&nbsp;a statement, you can delete transactions you think should be kept private).</li>
</ul>
<h3>Unlawful discrimination in renting</h3>
<p>In Victoria, it&rsquo;s against the law to stop somebody from renting a property because of certain personal characteristics, including:</p>
<ul>
    <li>age</li>
    <li>carer status, family responsibilities, parental status</li>
    <li>disability</li>
    <li>employment</li>
    <li>gender identity, lawful sexual activity, sexual orientation</li>
    <li>marital status</li>
    <li>physical features</li>
    <li>pregnancy, breastfeeding</li>
    <li>profession, trade or occupation (including being a sex worker)</li>
    <li>race (including colour, nationality, ethnicity and ethnic origin)</li>
    <li>religious belief or activity</li>
    <li>sex.</li>
</ul>
<p>If a rental provider asks about personal protected attributes outlined in the <em>Equal Opportunity Act 2010</em> (for example, ethnicity, gender identity, disability), they must provide you with a written reason for requesting this information.</p>
<p>The rental provider must also provide you with a statement on discrimination as part of the application form so that you are aware of your rights.</p>
<p>Find more information about <a href="/housing/renting/legal-and-dispute-support/unlawful-discrimination">unlawful discrimination in renting</a>.</p>
<p class="help-tips"><strong>Important to know:</strong> Rental providers or agents can&rsquo;t charge a fee to show you a property to rent.</p>
    </div>

</div>

<div id="page_content_1_subl_middlecolumn_2" class="section accordion ">
        
    <a class="accordion-toggle collapsed" aria-controls="{1A812AA0-7E8C-4E53-9072-3C76F2530D54}" aria-expanded="false" href="#">
        <h3 class="heading">Rules for advertised rental properties</h3>
    </a>              
        
    <div id="{1A812AA0-7E8C-4E53-9072-3C76F2530D54}" class="accordion-content">
        <h4>Legal requirements:</h4>
<ul>
    <li>Rental properties must be advertised at a fixed amount only. For example,&nbsp;$475 per week.</li>
    <li>It&rsquo;s unlawful for rental providers or agents to:
    <ul>
        <li>advertise a property with a price range</li>
        <li>ask for, invite or accept offers of rent higher than the advertised price</li>
        <li>ask for, invite or accept more than one month&rsquo;s rent in advance</li>
        <li>advertise or offer a property to let unless they reasonably believe the premises meet the rental minimum standards.</li>
    </ul>
    </li>
</ul>
<h3>Seen a rental property that doesn&rsquo;t look quite right?</h3>
<p>You can make an anonymous report to our Renting Taskforce using&nbsp;our&nbsp;<a href="/housing/renting/starting-and-changing-rental-agreements/applying-signing-and-moving-in/applying-for-a-property/report-an-issue-with-an-advertised-rental-property">online form</a>.</p>
<p>Since 2024, our Renting Taskforce has helped protect Victorian renters&rsquo;&nbsp;rights by taking action against rental providers and agents who break&nbsp;the law.</p>
<p>The taskforce investigates issues affecting the safety, security and&nbsp;wellbeing of renters, including:</p>
<ul>
    <li>advertising or renting out a property that doesn&rsquo;t meet the&nbsp;<a href="/housing/renting/repairs-alterations-safety-and-pets/minimum-standards/minimum-standards-for-rental-properties">rental minimum standards</a></li>
    <li>false advertising</li>
    <li>not lodging bonds with the <a href="/about-us/statutory-bodies-and-committees/residential-tenancies-bond-authority">Residential Tenancies Bond Authority</a>&nbsp;or taking an excessive bond (bonds should generally be no higher than the amount of one month's rent)</li>
    <li>re-letting a property within 6 months after using a notice to vacate claiming the property was going to be sold, demolished or converted</li>
    <li>failure to provide a condition report</li>
    <li>rental bidding.</li>
</ul>
<p>Rental providers and agents who break the law may be issued with&nbsp;a fine or warning or other enforcement action.</p>
    </div>

</div>

<div id="page_content_1_subl_middlecolumn_3" class="section accordion ">
        
    <a class="accordion-toggle collapsed" aria-controls="{1F457BA4-2950-4D30-881A-7C18E6DE4430}" aria-expanded="false" href="#">
        <h3 class="heading">Plan your budget and calculate rent payments</h3>
    </a>              
        
    <div id="{1F457BA4-2950-4D30-881A-7C18E6DE4430}" class="accordion-content">
        <p>Our free rent calculator can help you:</p>
<ul>
    <li>work out if a rental property fits your budget over time</li>
    <li>calculate the exact amount you owe if you move out part-way through a rent payment period</li>
    <li>plan your housing expenses more effectively.</li>
</ul>
<p>Use the&nbsp;<a href="/housing/renting/rent-bond-bills-and-condition-reports/rent/rent-calculator">rent calculator</a>.</p>
    </div>

</div>

<div id="page_content_1_subl_middlecolumn_4" class="section accordion last">
        
    <a class="accordion-toggle collapsed" aria-controls="{CE95C320-4B9C-4568-B0C9-53396B5A8B59}" aria-expanded="false" href="#">
        <h3 class="heading">Rental application checklist</h3>
    </a>              
        
    <div id="{CE95C320-4B9C-4568-B0C9-53396B5A8B59}" class="accordion-content">
        <p>This checklist includes the key steps when applying&nbsp;for a rental property.</p>
<p>
</p>
<ul class="checklist">
    <li><img alt="" src="/media/images/checklist-print.png" width="23" height="23" />Read through and complete the <a href="/library/forms/housing-and-accommodation/renting/residential-rental-application-31-march-2026.docx">rental application form</a>. </li>
    <li><img alt="" src="/media/images/checklist-print.png" width="23" height="23" />Provide the relevant identification and financial documents. </li>
    <li><img alt="" src="/media/images/checklist-print.png" width="23" height="23" />Make sure you can pay the bond (usually the same amount&nbsp;as one month&rsquo;s rent) along with the first month&rsquo;s rent.&nbsp;Generally, these need to be paid before you move in. </li>
    <li><img alt="" src="/media/images/checklist-print.png" width="23" height="23" />Sign your completed application form, include all required&nbsp;attachments and send these back to the agent or rental provider. </li>
</ul>
    </div>

</div>
